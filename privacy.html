Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information Ceylon Gem Lanka collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have a question about it, contact us on WhatsApp at +94 71 102 7938 or +1 (402) 808-7890.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password when you create an account. Your password is stored in a scrambled form that we cannot read.
  • Order details: your delivery address, district, phone numbers, the gems you buy, the amount you pay, and any note you add.
  • Payment slips: the photo or PDF of a bank transfer slip that you upload, so we can confirm your payment.
  • Card payments: if card payments are available and you use them, your card is processed on the secure payment page of our bank or payment provider. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form or on WhatsApp.
  • Information stored on your device: your cart, your sign-in, and your language choice are kept in your browser so the website works as you expect.

How we use your information

  • To process your order, confirm your payment and deliver your gems.
  • To contact you about your order, including on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • To answer your questions and give you the support you ask for.
  • To keep our records as the law requires.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners: your name, address and phone number, so they can deliver your order.
  • Payment providers: the details needed to process a card payment, if you choose to pay by card.
  • Service providers: the companies that host this website and our order system store your information on our behalf.
  • Authorities: when the law requires us to.

Translation

If you choose another language, or your browser uses one, the page text is translated by Google's website translator. The text of the page is sent to Google for translation.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you ask us to remove it. We keep order records for as long as we need them for delivery, support and our legal and accounting duties.

Your choices

You can see and change your details in My account. To get a copy of your information, correct it, or remove your account, contact us on WhatsApp.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page with a new date.
